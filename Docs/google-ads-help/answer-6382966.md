Official guide to Display campaigns.

![Best Practices logo](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/EB430F7808F7FA3402F7EAAB52709A42B586)

This guide offers Display campaign best practices for connecting customers with relevant ads across the web and apps. Get tips for setting up your campaign with effective ads, reaching the right audiences, and optimizing for performance.

#### **On this page**

* [1: Set your Display campaigns up for success](/google-ads/answer/6382966#success)
* [2: Reach the right audience at the right moment](/google-ads/answer/6382966#right-moment)
* [3: Create high-performing ads](/google-ads/answer/6382966#high-performing)
* [4: Measure the full value of Display](/google-ads/answer/6382966#measure)

| --- | --- |
| Gear icon | Set your Display campaigns up for success    * **Set up** [**conversion tracking**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167168)to ensure you're accurately tracking website and app conversions.   + We recommend that you check your conversion tracking regularly. You can also [test conversion tracking](https://support.google.com/google-ads/troubleshooter/10028419) by generating conversions and checking to see if they registered across Google Ads, Google Analytics, or your third-party platform. * **Select a** [**campaign goal**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7450050) **based on your objectives.** * **Use** [**Smart Bidding**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6167140) **to automate and optimize your bids.**   + Dynamic bids tailored to each individual auction can lead to better results.   Tip Display advertisers already using AI-powered features can now multiply results even further across channels with Performance Max. Advertisers who [upgrade](http://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13451710) Google Display campaigns to Performance Max see an average increase of over 20% in conversions at a similar or lower CPA.1 This performance holds even if they have existing Performance Max campaigns. Performance Max will help you reach valuable audiences more effectively across channels—including Display and beyond. |
| Conversion tracking icon | Reach the right audience at the right moment     * **Use** [**optimized targeting**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10538014)to discover which customers are more likely to convert within your campaign goals. * [Optimized targeting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10537509) is a privacy-safe tool that can help you reach your highest-value audience. By looking beyond manually-selected audience segments, optimized targeting finds relevant new users to help meet your campaign goals.   + **Add optional signals like audience segments or keywords** when using optimized targeting. Tip To re-engage existing customers with optimized targeting, include audience segments built with your data, like previous site visitors or repeat buyers. |
| Star icon | Create high-performing ads     * **Upload and maximize your assets (images, logos, videos, etc.) into** [**responsive display ads**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7005917) **for automatically generated ad combinations.** Tip Advertisers see 2x conversions, on average, when adding a responsive display ad to an ad group with a static display ad.2  Case study Bloomberg Media logo  By adopting responsive display ads, [Bloomberg Media](https://ads.google.com/home/success-stories/bloomberg-kepler) lowered their cost-per-action by 8% and cost-per-site-visit by 81%.  Responsive display ads by Bloomberg Media * **Use** [**advanced format options**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9848687)including asset enhancements, [auto-generated videos](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9848688), and native formats. * **Include your full catalog of products with relevant images, titles, and prices.** * **Test and optimize your display ads frequently** with fresh creative assets and images. |
| Bar chart | Measure the full value of Display   * **Consider all conversions while optimizing your campaigns**by including [engaged-view conversions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10048752) (EVCs) and [view-through conversions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6270625#:~:text=About%20view%2Dthrough%20conversions) (VTCs) when evaluating performance.   + These conversion types tell you when users see, but don’t interact with your ad, and then later complete a conversion on your site. This can be particularly helpful for measuring the influence of Display campaigns and can be viewed in the “Conversions” column. * **Check the** [**lookback windows**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3123169) **used by the conversions, VTCs or EVCs you’re tracking.** Depending on your business or product buying cycle, you might decide to adjust these settings from the default. * **Enable** [**GA4 auto-tagging**](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10723132)to ensure you get the most detailed Google Ads data. |
