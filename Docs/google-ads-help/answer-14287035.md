A guide on how to maximize creative performance using Google AI solutions

About creative performance
--------------------------

![](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/BSa5YBhh4sIVEtou5SLnrm7fn48swpkx115q)

Creative assets are more important to performance than ever before. According to NCS, your creative is responsible for 49% of the total sales impact of advertising.1And at every touch point, consumers expect highly relevant, personalized experiences from the brands they engage with.

AI-powered solutions can help deliver creative variations at the speed, quality, and quantity required to resonate with your customers today.

Follow these best practices below to maximize your creative.

Provide quality and quantity
----------------------------

![](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/BSa5YBhh4sIVEtou5SLnrm7fn48swpkx115q)

Give Google a variety of inputs in order to find out what works. From a strong website, to image assets or your business logo, ensure you share a range of on-brand creative elements.

* **Build a website with diverse, relevant landing pages that act as the source of truth for your brand**. Search and [Performance Max](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10724817) are able to pull information, such as your [business logo and name](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12497613),2as well as visuals to build new creative assets. It’s important the input from your website is accurate, specific, and recent, because outdated information can undermine your campaign.
* **Aim for quality *and*** **quantity:** Include a range of headlines and descriptions so Google AI can test different combinations and find what works best. This will help ensure each ad group in your Search campaign has at least one [responsive search ad](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791) with 'Good' or 'Excellent' Ad Strength.3
* **Add** [**image assets**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9566341)**4to your Search ads** to visually showcase your offerings, or enable [dynamic image assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10109688) to let Google AI automatically select the most relevant images from your landing pages and append them to your ads.

Generate at scale
-----------------

![](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/BSa5YBhh4sIVEtou5SLnrm7fn48swpkx115q)

Google AI helps you build out better assets, faster, and at scale. Accelerate the creative process by partnering with Google AI-powered solutions.

*Generate new:*

* **Enable** [**text customization**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11259373) **in both Search and Performance Max campaigns** to deliver more relevant ads. text customization will continually generate new headlines and descriptions that are better tailored to customers’ unique context and intent, while staying true to your business and offerings.
* **Use the** [**new asset generation**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14150602) **and generative AI capabilities in Performance Max.** You can generate new headlines, descriptions, and lifestyle images that are relevant and unique to your business—all right in the Google Ads UI.
* **Create a Search campaign**—with AI-generated suggestions for keywords, headlines, images, and more—**through chat**, with the [conversational experience in Google Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14145186).

*Augment existing assets:*

* **Use the** [**video creation tool**](https://ads.google.com/aw/video/builder) **to create a video in all orientations**: at least one vertical, one horizontal, and one square.5 Google AI intelligently flips your original video format and creates videos in new aspect ratios, and [Trim video](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12828451) can create Bumpers from longer clips.
* **Enhance your existing product imagery with** [**Product Studio**](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/13708167), **in Merchant Center Next.** It’s an easy way for you to update your product images, making them relevant for a variety of customers, product offers, and a variety of ad formats.

Evaluate and optimize
---------------------

![](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/BSa5YBhh4sIVEtou5SLnrm7fn48swpkx115q)

Understand your performance, iterate, and double down on what works. With the right insights, you can build a full understanding of which creative resonates with your audience.

* **Share top performing content and** [**asset audience insights**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/11372871)with creative teams to start strong. Both your creative teams, and Google AI, will use these to create new assets for testing.
* **Combine your** [**first-party data with Google AI**](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/future-of-marketing/privacy-and-trust/build-customer-relationships-and-trust/) to develop more powerful creative insights and improve the quality of your assets, which can lead to better performance. For example: your creative might perform better if they’re tailored to the stage of the purchase funnel your buyer is at, or even the region they see your ad in.
* **Keep an eye on** [**Ad Strength**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9142254)**.** Ad Strength provides you with forward-looking feedback that helps you understand how well an ad follows best practices for optimal performance and which action items to take to improve them.6
* **Use the** [**Insights page**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10256472) to understand new trends in performance and [run experiments](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6261395) on new ideas. This will help your brand put its best foot forward at all times across both evergreen and seasonal creative.

#### Customer Success Story:

![Armani Beauty logo](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/wiG5UXiVEGIT9kgQaAnwCpu9gJMBArE8WFea)

Armani beauty, part of L’Oreal’s portfolio of brands, was looking for ways to drive more on-site conversions with relevant and engaging Search ads. To reach more potential customers with relevant ads, the team added responsive search ads to each ad group of its unbranded campaigns while ensuring they had “Good” or “Excellent” Ad Strength. They also added assets, like sitelinks and images, to each ad group to help potential customers take action faster. As a result, Armani saw a 61% increase in click-through rate and 11% increase in on-site conversions.
