Best practices for getting the most out of your account on the go.

![A woman looking at her laptop. Next to her are 3 icons: upward arrow, list, and checkmark.](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/xUlSzZ4OteGMgpmUQi11J5EpejhYrQaFlhbO)

![Best Practices logo](//storage.googleapis.com/support-kms-prod/EB430F7808F7FA3402F7EAAB52709A42B586)

With the Google Ads mobile app, in addition to creating and monitoring your campaigns in real time with custom notifications, you can also review high-impact recommendations to improve performance and take action quickly and easily.

#### Tip

Make sure you’ve updated to the most recent version of the Google Ads mobile app. You can check for updates on [Google Play](https://ads.gle/links/2Kz1tmP3o6uQnbFa8) or the [Apple App Store](https://ads.gle/links/UBMgUev1NpA8SSDL6).

* [2: Take action to improve your account](/google-ads/answer/9470736#improve)
* [3: Keep your campaigns on track](/google-ads/answer/9470736#on-track)

| Mobile phone icon | Monitor performance    * **Use the account Overview page**to view your latest account performance and see if any action is needed. * **Review performance data**at the level that’s most relevant to you. You can [view performance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/4597811) of accounts, campaigns, ad groups, ads, and keywords. |
| Bar chart | Take action to improve your account    * **Check your r**[**ecommendations**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3416396)at least once a week. Google Ads surfaces recommendations based on real-time data, which you can review and apply quickly.   Tip Use [optimization score](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9061546) to prioritize recommendations. Optimization score [recommends actions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3416396) that have high potential for impact based on historical performance, auction simulations, and Google AI.  Examples of actions you can take include:  * Need to launch a campaign while in a taxi? [Pause or enable campaigns and ad groups](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/4597627). * Need to capture more opportunity over the weekend? [Set a budget for your campaign](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2375420). * Need to launch a last minute holiday promotion? [Create and edit responsive search ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9438230). |
| Conversion tracking icon | Keep your campaigns on track     * **Set up** [**custom notifications**](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10223911)from the Settings page**.** You can get real-time notifications and alerts—such as your weekly performance summary or ad disapprovals. * **Use the Help page in the app**to contact an expert via phone, chat, or email if you need support.   Tip When using the Google Ads app on the go, keep your accounts secure by setting a screen lock and 2-step verification. |
