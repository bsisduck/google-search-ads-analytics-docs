# SEO & Discoverability Audit — `app/index.html`

**Overall score: 75 / 100**

> **Context matters here.** `app/index.html` is a **local, internal Mermaid dashboard** — a `file://` snapshot served at `localhost:8800` that visualizes a Google-docs knowledge base and a recommended AI-agent architecture (`lang="pl"`, single page). It is an internal tool, **not a public marketing page**. For its current use, crawlability, indexing, rich results, hreflang and analytics are genuinely **not needed**, so most "missing" SEO signals are non-issues *in context*. The score reflects **deploy-readiness**, not failure: the page is technically clean (one H1, good title, no `noindex`), and the few real action items are cheap and only matter *if* the dashboard is ever published. The single most consequential deploy-time risk is the inverse of a normal SEO problem — an internal dashboard left publicly crawlable could be **accidentally indexed**.

## 4. What's likely NOT needed (internal dashboard)

As long as `app/index.html` stays an internal `localhost:8800` tool, the following are **genuinely not required** — they are deployment-time artifacts that only become relevant if the page is ever published to a public, indexable URL:

- **robots.txt, sitemap, canonical, HTTP-status/redirect checks, HTTPS/HSTS** — all live-URL concerns; nothing to do on a `file://` snapshot.
- **Structured data / JSON-LD / rich results** — no rich-result type maps to an internal architecture diagram; adding markup just to "have it" would violate Google's visible-content rule.
- **hreflang / multilingual handling** — single-language `pl` page; hreflang is optional even for multilingual sites and has no relationships to express here.
- **GA4 / GTM / Google Ads tags** — usage analytics on an internal tool is optional and often undesirable; the absence is the correct, privacy-respecting default.
- **CWV tuning for SEO** — Google itself says good CWV doesn't guarantee ranking; optimize only for the dashboard's actual users (load speed / offline reliability), not for Search.

The **one action that genuinely matters before any public deployment** is the inverse of normal SEO: add `noindex` (and/or authentication) so this internal dashboard is **not accidentally indexed** by Google.
